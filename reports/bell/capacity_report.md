## Realistic vs. theoretical

Theoretical capacity assumes every lot is rebuilt to the maximum. The realistic estimate counts only lots that pass every redevelopment screen (see METHODOLOGY.md), and only their *local* capacity: adding an ADU or a second house under state law usually keeps the existing home, and single-family zones are left out (see below).

- Net new homes, theoretical (all rules incl. state law): **11,011**
- Net new homes on lots **likely to redevelop**: **5,586** on 889 lots (21.3% of modeled lots)
- Single-family zones (SB 9, ADUs): **1,527** new homes in theory on 520 lots. Not counted as likely: small-scale infill on single-family lots has produced few homes wherever it's been allowed, whether through state law or a city reform.
