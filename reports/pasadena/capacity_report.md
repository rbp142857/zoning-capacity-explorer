# Capacity report: Pasadena, California

Generated by `make capacity`. Do not edit by hand.

> Estimates for research and advocacy, not legal or zoning advice. These are *theoretical* maximums under the rules as modeled; most lots will never be rebuilt (see the realistic estimate in milestone 7).

## Headline numbers

- Lots: **30,198**; modeled: **28,472** (94.3%); not yet modeled: **1,726** (specific-plan-only districts and planned developments)
- Existing homes on modeled lots: **60,681**
- Maximum legal homes on modeled lots, **local zoning only**: **72,497**
- Maximum legal homes **with state law** (SB 9, ADUs, AB 2097): **132,610**
- Net new capacity (max legal minus existing, never below 0 per lot): **79,505**
- Homes allowed by right (no use permit), where confirmed: **132,610**; zone's approval path confirmed on **28,472** of 28,472 modeled lots (100.0%). On other lots, only SB 9 homes count.
- SB 79, as a separate path: **4,587** lots could reach **104,232** homes, before local delay plans, tenant history and affordability limits. Not included above.
- 50% density bonus, as a separate path: **3,071** lots could reach **69,933** homes with 24% lower income or 15% very low income homes. Not included above.
- Share of residential land where **local zoning allows only one home**: **73%** (before state law; with SB 9 and ADU law almost none of it is limited to one home)

## Realistic vs. theoretical

Theoretical capacity assumes every lot is rebuilt to the maximum. The realistic estimate counts only lots that pass every redevelopment screen (see METHODOLOGY.md), and only their *local* capacity: adding an ADU or a second house under state law usually keeps the existing home, and single-family zones are left out (see below).

- Net new homes, theoretical (all rules incl. state law): **79,505**
- Net new homes on lots **likely to redevelop**: **10,729** on 1,106 lots (3.9% of modeled lots)
- Single-family zones (SB 9, ADUs): **53,639** new homes in theory on 20,134 lots. Not counted as likely: small-scale infill on single-family lots has produced few homes wherever it's been allowed, whether through state law or a city reform.

## Assumptions

- Average new unit: 850 sf inside the unit; 80% of building floor area is inside units, so each unit uses 1,062 sf of building
- Parking: surface parking, 300 sf of ground per stall including aisles
- Every zone-specific assumption is written next to its value in `cities/pasadena/rules/*.yaml`.
