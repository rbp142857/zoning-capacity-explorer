# Coverage: which cities are modeled, and how far to trust them

Generated by `python -m zce release`. Do not edit by hand. The same data is in [coverage.csv](coverage.csv).

**Levels** are simple thresholds, not a measure of accuracy. **Fuller:** 95%+ of lots modeled, 80%+ with approval type confirmed, zoning map from 2021 or newer (or the city's own). **Usable:** 85%+ of lots modeled. **Partial:** under 85%, usually because the plans where a city builds most are missing, so totals run low. No level accounts for bonus programs such as LA's TOC, which aren't modeled anywhere.

Today: 14 Fuller, 32 Usable, 9 Partial, of 55 cities with rule files (88 in the county). Avalon is rated Usable but held off the map, because wildlife restrictions on Catalina Island limit development and aren't modeled.

**Lots reviewed** counts lots in `docs/HAND_CHECKS.md`. In recent cities these review engine results rather than redo the arithmetic independently. **Special land** is government, utility, quarry, cemetery, golf and private school land, which counts in theoretical totals but not in likely new homes.
