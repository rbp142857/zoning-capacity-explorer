## Realistic vs. theoretical

Theoretical capacity assumes every lot is rebuilt to the maximum. The realistic estimate counts only lots that pass every redevelopment screen (see METHODOLOGY.md), and only their *local* capacity: adding an ADU or a second house under state law usually keeps the existing home, and single-family zones are left out (see below).

- Net new homes, theoretical (all rules incl. state law): **6,304**
- Net new homes on lots **likely to redevelop**: **236** on 38 lots (1.0% of modeled lots)
- Single-family zones (SB 9, ADUs): **3,178** new homes in theory on 1,101 lots. Not counted as likely: small-scale infill on single-family lots has produced few homes wherever it's been allowed, whether through state law or a city reform.
- Public or special land (government, utility, quarry, cemetery, golf, private school): **18** of the theoretical new homes, on 311 lots. Not counted as likely. They may rarely be rebuilt, so read citywide theoretical totals with this in mind.
